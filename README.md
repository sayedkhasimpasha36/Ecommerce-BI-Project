# AI-Powered E-Commerce Business Intelligence & Decision Support Dashboard

## Project Title
AI-Powered E-Commerce Business Intelligence & Decision Support Dashboard

---

## Problem Statement
E-commerce businesses generate large volumes of transactional data covering sales, customers, products, regions, pricing, discounts, and profitability. Raw transactional data alone does not provide clear guidance for business decisions. This project transforms a real-world e-commerce dataset into an interactive Business Intelligence and Decision Support Dashboard that delivers meaningful KPIs, trends, risk signals, opportunities, and actionable business recommendations.

---

## Objectives
- Load, clean, and validate the global e-commerce sales dataset without modifying the original file.
- Compute accurate KPIs including Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin, Average Order Value, and Average Discount.
- Identify top-performing and underperforming products, categories, regions, and customer segments.
- Detect loss-making orders, high-discount impacts, and revenue concentration risks.
- Provide a rule-based AI Business Analyst section that summarises findings, risks, and recommended actions from calculated data only.
- Deliver an interactive, filter-driven dashboard accessible to non-technical business managers.

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| `Order_ID` | Unique order identifier |
| `Order_Date` | Date the order was placed (YYYY-MM-DD) |
| `Customer_Name` | Full name of the customer |
| `Customer_Segment` | Customer type: Consumer, Corporate, or Home Office |
| `Country` | Country of the order |
| `Region` | Geographic region (Asia Pacific, Europe, Middle East & Africa, North America, South America) |
| `Product_Category` | Category: Technology, Furniture, Office Supplies, Clothing & Accessories |
| `Product_Name` | Name of the product ordered |
| `Quantity` | Number of units ordered |
| `Unit_Price` | Price per unit ($) |
| `Discount_Percent` | Discount applied to the order (%) |
| `Total_Sales` | Revenue generated by the order ($) |
| `Shipping_Cost` | Cost of shipping for the order ($) |
| `Profit` | Profit reported for the order ($) — treated as provided; no COGS column exists |
| `Payment_Method` | Payment method used (Bank Transfer, Cash on Delivery, Credit Card, PayPal) |

## Key Insights (Full Dataset)
> These are example findings from the complete unfiltered dataset. Actual values update dynamically in the dashboard.

1. **Profit Margin** — The overall profit margin across 2,000 orders provides a baseline for performance monitoring.
2. **Category Leadership** — Technology and Clothing & Accessories tend to differ in margin profile; the dashboard quantifies this precisely.
3. **Loss-Making Orders** — A proportion of orders report negative profit; the dashboard isolates and details these for management review.
4. **Discount Impact** — Orders with discounts ≥ 20% show measurably different average margins versus low-discount orders.
5. **Regional Concentration** — Some regions contribute disproportionately to revenue, creating concentration risk.
6. **Customer Segments** — Corporate vs Consumer vs Home Office segments exhibit different profitability profiles.

## Limitations
- The `Profit` column is used as provided in the dataset. There is no COGS column, so profit cannot be independently recalculated.
- Shipping cost is available but there is no cost-of-goods or operating expense data beyond what is in the CSV.
- Customer-level lifetime value (LTV) analysis is limited by the absence of a persistent customer ID (Customer_Name is used as a proxy).
- No external market data is used; all insights are derived solely from the supplied CSV.
- The AI Business Analyst section is rule-based, not powered by a language model.
